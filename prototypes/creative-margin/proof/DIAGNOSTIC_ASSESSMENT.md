# Stage 1 diagnostic assessment — synthetic only

## What the analysis can and cannot say

The deterministic engine and project-type sensitivity are now implemented. The sensitivity groups only calculable projects, and uses an **illustrative**, not buyer-approved, 20% contribution margin. The arithmetic is: hypothetical total fee = known labour plus direct costs divided by 0.8. This assumes unchanged costs, volume, customer acceptance and scope. It is **not** money recoverable or a prediction of future sales.

The synthetic fixture does contain four negative-contribution jobs and eight recorded-hour overruns. These are useful investigation prompts, but do not establish a recurring quoting defect: no original staffing mix, approved scope baseline, detailed change orders, invoice timing or comparable-job matching exists. Project type labels alone are not valid controls for project complexity.

## Decision-quality gaps

- A conventional project management dashboard can already show hours versus budget. A buyer will not pay just to have that observation restated.
- Distinguishing **unpriced scope change** from **bad initial estimate** requires dated scope/approval events and labour allocation. The present fixture does not support this attribution.
- A fee-floor scenario is mathematically trivial if cost data is available. The commercial value would have to be in trustworthy reconciliation, attribution and a defensible next-quote recommendation.
- Missing rates/hours and uncertain retainer allocation block three project contributions. In a real agency, the cost of resolving these could dominate delivery.
- No real export sample, founder time study, buyer interview or paid demand exists.

## Gate

**Stage 1 commercial gate: NOT PASSED.** Technical feasibility is supported on synthetic records, but the distinctive purchase reason is not. Proceed with a bounded Stage 2 incumbent/substitution review and explicit acquisition/cost hypothesis; do not build a hosted product or claim commercial viability.

## Next proof required

A permissioned, anonymised 10–20-project export with change-order history and labour-cost methodology; time the cleaning and reconciliation; demonstrate at least one materially different next-quote decision that an existing dashboard or a simple spreadsheet would not produce. No real data should be uploaded before secure processing is approved.

# Project Profitability Review — fictional demonstration

> **Entirely synthetic.** Illustrative only; not an agency audit, verified client result, or evidence of buyer demand. All amounts EUR excluding VAT.

## Executive findings

- 20 completed fictional projects; 17 calculable, 3 blocked by missing or ambiguous information.
- Posted invoice revenue across all projects: **€327,700.00**. This is not a portfolio profit figure.
- Contribution for the **17 calculable projects only**: **€63,394.00** on **€264,700.00** revenue (23.9% contribution margin). The three blocked projects are excluded from both numerator and denominator.
- **4** calculable projects have negative contribution; **5** fall below an *illustrative* 20% contribution threshold. **8** projects have recorded hours above original planned hours. These counts are not claims of financial loss from overruns alone.

## Method

Posted invoice lines minus posted credits determine operational invoice revenue; drafts are excluded. Recorded project hours multiplied by known fully burdened rates determine labour cost. Posted direct vendor expenses are deducted once. **Contribution = revenue − labour − direct expenses**. Missing time/rates or unclear revenue allocation prevents a contribution result. Non-project time, shared overhead, tax, collections and audited revenue recognition are not modelled. Contribution is **not net profit**.

## Suggested owner actions

1. Reconcile the three blocked projects before using portfolio-wide margin numbers.
2. Review scope change handling and project estimates for overrun/loss projects; do not assume all overruns are unbilled.
3. Confirm retainer revenue allocation, draft invoices and missing time before adjusting rates or future quotes.
4. Set an agency-approved contribution target and fully burdened cost methodology before attempting pricing-floor sensitivity. No savings forecast is justified by this fictional data.

## Minimum inputs for a future real pilot

Project register/estimates; time entries with completeness attestations; fully burdened labour rate methodology; posted invoice and credit lines; direct vendor expenses; change orders; retainer allocation; agreed revenue policy and reconciliation contact. Do not provide real customer records to this prototype. Any live pilot requires permission, secure transfer, restricted access and deletion controls.

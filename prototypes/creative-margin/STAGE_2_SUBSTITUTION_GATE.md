**Status:** Research/validation required. This document is a structured competitive hypothesis, not a verified feature-by-feature market audit. Do not treat vendor capability or pricing as confirmed without primary-source verification.

## Buyer and job hypothesis

Buyer: owner/managing director or operations lead of a 5–50-person creative agency using disconnected time, invoicing and project records. The job is **a bounded retrospective decision review** of 10–20 completed jobs, with traceable evidence and a shortlist of next-quote controls. Not a replacement for an agency operating system.

## Conditional offer (not yet sellable)

- Input: project IDs, approved estimates and change orders, posted invoices/credits, timesheets and burdened labour costs, direct vendor expenses, retainer allocation.
- Output: reconciled contribution with unresolved exceptions, attributable variance categories only where source evidence exists, and a next-quote control checklist.
- Exclusions: accounting assurance, tax, cash-flow audit, guaranteed recoveries, forecasts, or ongoing project management.
- Delivery: local reproducible calculations, manual QA and concise written decision report.
- Pricing: **unknown**. Do not invent a price without an observed willingness-to-pay signal or defensible cost-to-deliver estimate.

## Founder-effort and recurring economics gate

Measure actual hours to request, normalise, reconcile and review each 10–20-job dataset. Let per-review price be P, direct delivery cost C and founder hours H. Monthly reviews required for €5,000 revenue = ceiling(5000/P), *not* profit. Test both acquisition cost and repeat-purchase cadence. One-off historical reviews do not automatically create monthly recurring revenue.

## Stage 2 kill questions

1. Can a buyer export the required records without hours of manual reconstruction?
2. Does the report provide at least one materially new, defensible next-quote decision, beyond existing reports?
3. Will a buyer pay for a separate retrospective review when their software or accountant already offers similar analysis?
4. Can founder hours per engagement be bounded with a repeatable process?
5. What is the ethical and credible buyer-acquisition channel, given weak cold-outreach response in other experiments?

**Current recommendation:** Do not build the landing page or deploy yet. First verify competitive overlap and real input feasibility. A polished page would prematurely imply an established offer. Prospect contact and handling of customer data still require explicit approval.

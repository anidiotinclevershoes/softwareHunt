# Stage 2 verified competitor review — 14 October 2026

Sources are vendors' own pages, not independent capability testing or proof of buyers' willingness to purchase from us.

## Primary-source findings

1. **Parakeeto** offers a one-off Profit Assessment starting at US$4,999, plus ongoing Profit Management starting at US$1,499/month. Its stated target is agencies/consultancies with at least US$1m annual revenue. Assessment includes historical analysis, KPI modelling, benchmarking and a prioritised roadmap. Parakeeto explicitly describes its internal software as consolidating, cleaning and reporting data from clients' existing systems. Sources: https://www.parakeeto.com/pricing/ ; https://www.parakeeto.com/services/agency-profitability-assessment/ ; https://www.parakeeto.com/faq/
2. **Productive** already offers budgets, time tracking, profitability by client, budget usage and cost-aware time entry reports; its published guidance includes estimate-versus-actual review. Sources: https://productive.io/industries/agency-management-software/ ; https://productive.io/blog/agency-project-management-guide/
3. **Scoro** markets integrated quoting, budget, supplier bills, time, invoices and real-time profitability. Its published agency case study describes detailed quote-versus-actual reconciliation and scope-change workflows. Sources: https://www.scoro.com/apps/estimating-and-budgeting/ ; https://www.scoro.com/blog/case-study-how-a-multi-entity-agency-saved-2-days-on-reporting
4. **Spreadsheet + AI assistant** remains a plausible low-cost substitute. No timed independent test has yet been run.

## What this invalidates

- Profitability calculations or project loss lists are not differentiated.
- Combining exports and reporting financial metrics is not a novel technology-enabled service; Parakeeto openly operates this model.
- A generic 10–20-project retrospective review overlaps existing software and consultancy offerings.
- A €5k/month revenue goal is not a substitute for evidence of pricing power, lead acquisition and repeat purchase.

## Possible residual wedge — unvalidated

A tightly fixed-price, fast-turnaround **quote-error forensic review** for sub-US$1m-revenue agencies using disconnected tools and unable/unwilling to buy a full agency operating platform or US$4,999+ consultancy assessment.

Potential output: three source-reconciled, evidence-backed controls for the next comparable quotation, with explicit separation of original estimate error, scope change and time-recording error. The present fixture cannot do this attribution. Pricing and turnaround are **not yet established**.

## Kill gates

1. Find evidence that the smaller agency segment experiences this exact unresolved problem and has budget to pay for a standalone review.
2. Obtain representative export schemas and demonstrate reproducible attribution with minimal founder involvement.
3. Run spreadsheet/AI substitution against the same dataset, timed and scored for accuracy.
4. Establish customer willingness to pay **before** hosting software or committing to recurring revenue claims.
5. Check whether Parakeeto or other specialist providers already sell a smaller-budget equivalent.

## Decision

**NO-GO for the original broad product/service positioning. CONDITIONAL DISCOVERY ONLY for the narrow forensic wedge.** No outreach, spending, deployment or handling of real customer data authorised.

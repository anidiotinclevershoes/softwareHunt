## Primary anomaly — #761 outsourced electrical as-built CAD revision
**Payer / purchase trigger:** electrical/M&E contractors with redlined drawings and sign-off pressure. The UK/Jersey provider [Outsource CAD](https://www.outsourcecad.com/resources/as-built-drawings-cost) quotes approximately **£50–£80 per drawing**. Its named [United Electrical Contractors case](https://www.outsourcecad.com/case-study/united-electrical-contractors) says outsourced as-built revisions and electrical layouts persisted across **10+ projects and over a year**. Independent reviews [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/outsourcecad.com) support that paying external CAD services exist; neither independently certifies the case-study project count nor what each customer paid. **M3; S3 for demonstrated independently bought external digital completion, not evidence of entrant selection.**

**Exact proposed bought result:** take customer's source DWG, construction markup PDF or scan, issue **corrected editable DWG + reviewable PDF + revision schedule**, with changes verified against provided marks. A markup-summary or AI diagram would *not* count.

**Feasibility / killer:** [Autodesk](https://www.autodesk.com/support/technical/article/caas/sfdcarticles/sfdcarticles/How-to-automate-as-built-drawing-updates-from-a-marked-up-PDF-in-AutoCAD.html) expressly says AutoCAD can partially ingest markup, but **final drawings normally require manual drafting review**. DWG authoring via [ODA SaaS SDK licence](https://www.opendesign.com/oda-membership) may begin around **$7,500 first year**. So the paid result is real, but low-touch independent full-job automation is **unproven**; human-per-drawing fallback could destroy solo-founder economics. C2–C3 competitor; D2 Autodesk procedures but **no accepted auto-correction oracle**.

**Why separate entrant might theoretically be selected:** high-volume, repetitive small revision jobs for electrical subcontractors could be packaged and completed quicker with safe refusal and an objectively checked CAD diff. **This is a hypothesis**, not demonstrated buyer switch or product fit. Distribution: electrical subcontractor/contractor directories and named clients of current outsource providers, but **ten genuinely matching first buyers not verified**. No outreach-ready promotion.

**Cheapest falsification:** obtain permissioned representative before/after DWGs and annotated markups for 10–20 completed tasks; classify geometry/text/symbol changes, document verification criteria, measure percentage *actually automatically completable* without a CAD drafter. Only after positive result test payment/selection with contractors. **ANOMALY ONLY**; no build authorised.

## Banked evidence — #762 Irish lighting company keeps buying outsourced catalogue pages
[Channel Studios' named ROBUS case](https://www.channelstudios.co.uk/case-studies/robus) reports ROBUS continued outsourcing catalogue-page production **even after** implementing PIM-to-InDesign/AutoPub automatic page assembly. This is exactly the kind of paid coexistence V3.6 seeks. **But** supplier's case is not independently confirmed buyer spend and invoices are undisclosed. Mature integrated publishing and [EasyCatalog](https://www.65bit.com/software/easycatalog/pricing/) already handle many relevant transformations. The customer's remaining paid purchase may be layout review, exception handling and project management, i.e. **human production**, not a standalone software output. M3 named seller/customer testimony, S2; C2. **BANKED LEAD only**, not an outreach proposition. The next decisive question is whether any manufacturer has a recurring separable, verifiably finished catalogue batch that a new vendor can produce without PIM migration or graphic-designer QA.

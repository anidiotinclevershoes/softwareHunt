## Commercial decision
**Stop the broad comparison now.** It is sufficient to commit to a **two-segment cash test**, not a new formal board promotion:
1. Creative agencies first: validate whether a business will commission a **small fixed-scope historical margin review** without replacing PSA. Need real data readiness, useful answer beyond Scoro/Productive/Parakeeto, an actual fixed fee commitment and <= founder time budget. Appropriate source companies from Ireland IAPI + UK DBA, not a cold automated blast.
2. Separate segment **recurring service-contract providers** only if agencies cannot show the spend/selection need *or* pilot model repeats efficiently. Same analytics engine, different customer input and sales motion: avoid simultaneous two bespoke products.
Keep contractor documents as **fallback offer** with distinct non-professional-review acceptance. CPO separately, with existing six sends and no new unsolicited mail.

**Reason for limit:** more researching old banking lists has diminishing evidence value; direct buyer selection is now the bottleneck. No outreach, software build, spend, deploy, merge or prior status changed.

## Deep anomaly attacks

### 1. Podcast ad delivery/make-good closure — KILLED

- **Observed job:** insertion order and approved copy → scheduled episodes/placements → server-side delivery evidence → missed/incorrect placement → make-good or credit → invoice closure. IAB UK documents fragmented, server-side measurement and cross-publisher comparability problems.
- **Buyer/access:** [Podnews' public agency directory](https://podnews.net/directory/category/ad-agencies) and UK specialists such as Priority Sounds make agencies identifiable. This proves contactability, not a qualified first ten or unmet spend.
- **Competitor attack:** [LiveRead](https://www.liveread.io/) covers IOs, automatic impression delivery, billing and bill reconciliation; [Flightpath](https://www.flightpath.fm/) forecasts inventory, flags under-performing campaigns and consolidates campaign/financial data. An AI-agent offer explicitly sells make-good closure at $149/month.
- **AI test:** with IOs, placement logs, delivery exports and invoices, an agent can build the exception ledger and draft make-good actions. Reliable automation still needs multiple ad-server integrations—the same burden incumbents already carry.
- **Second kill:** a CSV-first utility is easily substituted and capped near commodity pricing; a dependable product becomes an integration-heavy ad-ops platform competing with exact incumbents. No evidence supports £500–£1,000/month for a narrower UK entrant.
- **Verdict:** kill; no outreach.

### 2. Grassroots football registration-error guard — KILLED

- **Observed job/consequence:** club registration data and deadlines determine eligibility. A reported Scottish case involved a 15-point deduction and £500 fine after COMET registration problems.
- **Buyer/access:** clubs are public, but administrators are often volunteers. [SAFA now offers one-to-one video and telephone COMET support](https://scottishamateurfa.co.uk/comet-video-call-support-launched/), demonstrating pain and also an incumbent human substitute.
- **Competitor attack:** COMET is the authoritative registration system; SportOS advertises player eligibility and league registration at £0/£38/£70 per month. Other club platforms bundle registration, payments and fixtures.
- **AI test:** AI can turn rules/screenshots into a checklist and spot missing fields, but cannot certify the authoritative COMET state without integration or association data access.
- **Second kill:** the apparent product either remains an unauthoritative checklist with a very low price ceiling or needs COMET integration/endorsement and support. Volunteer clubs cannot justify the preferred £500–£1,000 monthly price.
- **Verdict:** kill; the penalty case does not establish a standalone business.

### 3. Irish Foreign Birth Registration dossier — KILLED

- **Observed job:** eligibility route → birth/marriage/death and ID evidence across up to three generations/jurisdictions → certified copies and address evidence → online form plus physical originals. The official adult fee is €278; incomplete/clarification cases take longer.
- **Current spend:** public services advertise roughly £300–£649 for assisted/full-service applications; the previously found genealogist advertises £300–£500 end-to-end. This is service spend, not SaaS willingness to pay.
- **Competition:** Ancestral Trails provides research, retrieval and checking; Get Irish Passport sells a £649 full service; Irish Citizenship Helper includes a dossier account; [Rootdossier](https://rootdossier.app/) is the exact document-organiser proposition.
- **AI test:** for straightforward routes, official checklists plus AI can map people/documents and surface name/date mismatches. Hard cases require genealogy, certification, foreign record procurement and judgment.
- **Second kill:** consumer use is once-only, trust/support load is high, and acquisition competes with official guidance and full-service advisers. A £149 transaction would require about 34 monthly sales for €5k monthly revenue before acquisition/support; that is arithmetic, not demand evidence.
- **Verdict:** kill as standalone software; no outreach.

### 4. Pawnbroker surplus-return prompt — KILLED AS STANDALONE

- **Observed job/consequence:** after sale of an unredeemed pledge, net proceeds above redemption value remain payable to the customer. [FCA research](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/research-notes/more-reminders-empowering-pawnbroking-staff-better-customer-outcomes) reports over £1m annually unclaimed in a small 2018 sample; a better letter nearly doubled 30-day collection and a store-side flag improved collection nearly 44%.
- **Buyer/access:** the [National Pawnbrokers Association](https://www.thenpa.com/About-the-NPA.aspx) says it represents 97% of the trade and more than 1,200 outlets, with a public finder. This is the clearest real customer-access evidence in the batch.
- **Competition/incumbent:** FCA rules require pledge/sale/surplus/payment records inside the regulated books. Existing pawnbroker POS such as NosPOS already handles agreements, renewals and post-expiry reminders; the successful FCA intervention was itself a flag inside one large firm's system.
- **AI test:** AI can improve letter/message copy and segment outreach from an export. It cannot safely become the system of record without POS integration, customer identity controls and regulated audit history.
- **Second kill:** the total harm is meaningful but was estimated from a small sample and is spread across chains/outlets. The winning intervention is a low-cost incumbent feature, not an independent truth layer. A standalone product would require sensitive integrations and regulated support; selling to chains creates enterprise procurement.
- **Verdict:** kill standalone SaaS; the product opportunity belongs to existing POS vendors or an FCA/NPA implementation campaign.

### 5. Synthetic-voice consent/licensing ledger — KILLED FOR CURRENT FOUNDER FIT

- **Observed job:** explicit consent → project/media/territory/term restrictions → approvals and remuneration → provenance → audit/withdrawal → suspected misuse evidence. [Equity](https://www.equity.org.uk/advice-and-support/know-your-rights/ai-toolkit/equitys-ai-vision-statement) explicitly calls for consent, time-limited personal licences, transparent information and fair remuneration.
- **Buyer/access:** UK voice agencies, casting firms, games studios and audio-post houses are identifiable through directories; Easy Tiger Voices publishes a direct agency contact. This establishes reachability, not a first-ten willingness to pay.
- **Competitor attack:** [VoiceSeal](https://voiceseal.io/) already offers biometric enrolment, consent checks, licensing, C2PA provenance and evidence reports; talicense and TalentRights claim cross-platform detection, rights issuance and enforcement workflows.
- **AI test:** AI makes contract extraction, licence comparison and takedown drafting cheap. The non-commodity value is reliable biometric matching, platform coverage, provenance adoption, payments and legal evidence—far beyond a weekend build.
- **Second kill:** a simple consent register has little enforcement value; a valuable system needs marketplace/network effects, biometric security, false-positive management, regulatory/legal operations and integrations with synthesis platforms. Exact 2026 entrants are already pursuing that full stack.
- **Verdict:** kill the lightweight entrant; do not bank as a live candidate absent direct UK agency demand for a sharply bounded tool competitors demonstrably lack.

## Commercial decision

- **New-round survivors:** none.
- **Promotions/banked items:** none. All five anomalies failed competitor, AI-substitution and separate bear-case gates.
- **Commercial pattern:** the batch produced several real incidents and paid jobs, but deterministic layers were again owned by official portals, low-priced vertical products or new 2026 entrants. Residual value sat in physical work, expert judgement, integrations, regulated records or one-off support.
- **Customer access:** real public access exists for pawnbrokers (NPA finder/1,200+ outlets), podcast agencies (Podnews directory), Scottish football clubs/administrators (SAFA/association routes) and voice agencies/studios (industry directories). None combined accessible first-ten buyers with an unowned job and rational £500–£1,000/month spend.
- **Board:** unchanged. CPO remains OUTREACH-READY/not BUILD-READY; quarry/mineral assurance remains provisional DESK-VALIDATION/practitioner-test; GP income assurance remains DESK-VALIDATION with its dedicated outcome unrecovered. Existing kills remain killed.

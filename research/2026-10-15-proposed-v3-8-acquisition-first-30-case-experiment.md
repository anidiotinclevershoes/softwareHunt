# Proposed V3.8 — Acquisition-first 30-case experiment (research sidecar only)

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Status:** PROPOSED METHOD / USER-AUTHORISED EXPERIMENT; **not an approved permanent authority amendment**; supplement for this bounded exercise only. Existing V3–V3.7 gate, graveyard, frozen manifest and research branch unchanged.

## Motivation
The Market Entry Gradient (20 parent markets, 40 exploratory variants, 24 later screened propositions, automated implementation failed final gate) and subsequent 18-observation money-first scan revealed recurring problems: incumbent software is feature-rich/cheap, vendor pricing does not equal purchaser adoption, big contracts buy human/physical/proprietary capacity, and a feature sliver between competitors is commercially uninteresting. The previous method is good at killing but not yet good at spotting accessible customer purchases.

## User-approved experimental assumptions for the next bounded pass
- **10 active-buying cases**: actual buyer-authored requests, commissions, migration decisions or concrete near-term purchase signals. Search buyer evidence first; record quoted price/budget where public.
- **10 existing-paid-spend cases**: recurring small-business expenditure on software, purchased digital deliverables or outsourcing. Evidence M2–M4; do not infer that the entire fee is capturable.
- **10 divergent-entry cases**: authentic, unconventional purchase occasions, cross-category supply, new revenue offerings, existing third-party channels; prioritise Ireland then UK then EU/EEA.
- At discovery stage record buyer, transaction, stated/observable price, trigger, existing provider, route to first ten and **selection hypothesis** before performing full technical/competitor kill. It is permitted to mark a case *worth a cheap buyer experiment* on a selection hypothesis **only if** the standing V3.3 OUTREACH-READY rules are separately satisfied. Do **not** promote merely on a plausible difference.
- Distinguish current spend from *entrant capturable spend*: continue V3.6 S0–S4 separately from M0–M4; user direction widens search paths but does not weaken proof for outbound or build.
- Not all businesses need unique unoccupied niches: assess genuine competition, commercial headroom, broad buyer class, ability to win the first ten, then the full end-to-end outcome and support.
- Shortlist **at most 10** for commercial screening, **at most 5** for second kill, **at most 3** for outreach consideration (none required). Two targeted evidence probes on a missing primary fact, then mark **INCOMPLETE**, rather than invent.
- Explicitly exclude photo management and family organisation (including adjacent variants) from this *new* hunt; do not silently modify historical banked records. Do not resurrect previously killed workflows on terminology changes.
- **Stop** after the documented 30 cases and strongest challenges: don't pivot to ever-narrower feature gaps. Mark zero qualified candidates truthfully.
- Audit counts: each row is a distinct buyer + trigger + purchased outcome. Search queries or product variants are not automatically distinct counted cases; follow-up competitor searches aren't extra cases.
- Research **unnumbered**: not the previously frozen and unresearched #961–1060 random manifest. No outreach, spending, deployment, production code, or PR merge without express separate instruction.

## Evidence score labels
- M0–M4 spend (V3.5); S0–S4 capturability (V3.6); C0–C4 competitor strength (V3.1); D0–D4 documented implementability (V3.4).
- For early discovery, record C/D as **NOT YET CHECKED** where appropriate rather than extrapolating.
- Seller asking price, active posted buyer job, verified purchase/award, and entrant-specific paid commitment are distinct evidence kinds. A buyer posting a request is **not** a verified award/payment; a gov contract award is not actual disbursement.
- Recorded records must include source URLs, date, strongest kill, classification (no kill/weak/incomplete/directional kill), and cheapest decisive step.

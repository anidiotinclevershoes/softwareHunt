# Buyer-side tipping-point evidence 03 — 2026-10-14

Exploratory boundary research, not new certified V3 attempts or an authority amendment. Primary procurement source: HCPC 2018 audio recording and transcription invitation to tender https://www.hcpc-uk.org/globalassets/about-us/governance/procurement/hcpc1801/draft-recording-transcription-and-additional-services-framework.pdf .

## Primary economic artefact
HCPC reported **over GBP 490,000 annual expenditure** on recording and transcription and estimated GBP 2m across health regulators (PDF page 5, section 2.9). It already had two service providers, planned a four-supplier framework (2.1–2.2). It estimated transcript folios represented 56% of HCPC cost, with attendance fees another major component (2.12). It required on-site recording/logging, secure retention seven years, verbatim accuracy, three-day standard and one-day urgent transcript turnaround, booking and consolidated invoicing, quality assurance (2.14–2.16). Technical assessment weighted 55%, price 35%, interview 10% (15.1).

**Evidence level**: buyer-origin procurement documentation and historical stated spend; NOT proof of contract award, actual 2026 spend, or demand for a new standalone transcript AI product.

## Boundary finding
This is materially stronger than seller advertised price as evidence that buyers pay, but most contract scope includes presence, operational responsibility, data security and high-stakes QA. Transcription text alone is not necessarily a separable purchase. A transcript generator does not replace the contract. The 56% folio component is an **internal service cost allocation**, not 56% capturable software revenue.

## Updated tipping-point hypothesis
High verified buyer expenditure + clearly defined digital output still fails founder fit when contract bundles on-site execution, liability, secure handling and operational SLA. To identify an inside case, find buyer-side procurement of **the completed digital output alone**, without the inseparable on-site/service elements, plus documented verification and a viable independent supplier selection mechanism.

**Verdict:** 1 historically documented buyer-spend case, **outside software-only entrant boundary for this bundled scope**; 0 S3 independently paid software-only outputs established, 0 promotions. No changes to CPO, graveyard, frozen #961–1060 or V3 authority. Next: search award records for standalone digital transformations that buyers can accept against objective criteria.

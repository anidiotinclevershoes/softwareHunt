## Second kill
Independent tronc service is real (S2 service sold by identifiable providers), but the *complete purchased outcome* is legal/tax/payroll governance, not a simple allocation spreadsheet. The proposed software-only component lacks a separately documented buyer transaction (S1). Providers already offer software plus independent oversight. A cheap entrant must replace governance, accept operational exposure or win customers dissatisfied with a known vendor; none is supported by independent buyer evidence. No S3 buyer selection or qualifying first-ten list.

**Verdict: NOT OUTREACH-READY; NO BUILD.** Bank only as market evidence, not a qualified opportunity. Do not interpret vendors' claimed savings as verified savings. No graveyard promotion or outreach.

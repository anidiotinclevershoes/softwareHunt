## Adversarial second-kill on #779 (most promising spend)
- **Payer:** firm commissioning measured as-built Revit model for renovation/fit-out from point cloud already held. Quote per m², not an invoice or proof customers switch.
- **Full job:** accurate semantic BIM objects, family relationships, correct datum/co-ordinates, agreed LOD and verified surfaces. Providing only meshes or extracting wall-like planes is **not the purchased job**.
- **Documentation:** Autodesk ReCap can import/register/classify point clouds; FARO As-Built already extracts constrained objects. D2–D3 for parts of workflow. Geometry deviation can be validated against a declared tolerance; semantic accuracy/occluded structure remain ambiguous and need experienced technicians.
- **AI repeated-execution substitution:** general multimodal models cannot consistently interpret variable-quality large point clouds, infer pipe/door/wall relationships, generate editable correct Revit families and defend all exceptions without engineering review.
- **AI-build test:** vendor-grade geometric extraction already bundled inside FARO; a fresh company must compete on full output, maintain BIM versions and model exchange, and manage support/security for multi-gigabyte building scans.
- **Selection/first-ten:** identifiable architectural firms exist, but no ten verified buyers demanding independent unattended modelling and no evidence that software would supplant their survey-model service. Other professionals [report both in-house and outsource usage](https://www.reddit.com/r/Architects/comments/1c4240e), not product willingness.
- **Counter-case:** high service price is the labor bill for modelling and QC; one small automated feature cannot capture it. **BANKED as an economic observation only.** Cheapest honest falsification: permissioned before/after sample point clouds, approved IFC/RVT models, stated acceptance/deviation reports; test whether all required geometry/semantics can be completed without per-job CAD/BIM operator. **No outreach or prototype has been authorised or performed.**

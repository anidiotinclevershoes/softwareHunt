# 2026-10-13 — Actual late-filing spend and failure mechanism

**Scope:** eight buyer/payment/consequence trails and five second-kill checks on the user seeds Compliance, Deadline, Fine. These are follow-up discovery probes, not certified numbered attempts 761+. No survivors.

## Verified money
- CRO official 2025 annual report https://cro.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CRO-Annual-Report-2025-Final-19-June.pdf reports **15,163 companies paid late filing fees in 2025**, **average €572.54**; income table reports **€8.7m late-filing fees**. The arithmetic 15,163 × €572.54 is approximately €8.68m, consistent with the reported total. Also **1,422 notices of court-extension applications** received. This is M4-equivalent *statutory fees actually paid*, **not** customer spend on preventive SaaS or an addressable revenue forecast.
- Companies House independent adjudicators report 2024–25 https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-independent-adjudicators-report-2024-to-2025/annual-report-by-the-independent-adjudicators-to-companies-house-2024-to-2025 reports **317,985 late-account penalties issued** and **47,673 first-stage appeals**. Issued is **not paid**. Government management dataset https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/companies-house-management-information-april-2024-to-march-2025 .

## More specific preventable failure
CRO official filing guide https://cro.ie/annual-return/filing-an-annual-return/ says returned annual filings ordinarily have 14 days to cure defects, after which the original may be deemed never filed; **missing B1 signatures or missing/late financial statements can cause immediate rejection without that correction window**. This is more specific than forgetting a deadline: the deliverable needs complete signatures, financial statements, accepted submission and receipt. However, reliable checking of those components, timely coordination, and authority to file may require client/accountant involvement. CRO says company-secretarial filing software already exists and EFA may file with authorization but cannot certify financial statements on behalf of directors/secretary.

## Second kill and distribution
- CRO annual return first B1 service from €119+VAT, later annual-return+financial-statement package from €499+VAT https://www.incorpro.ie/annual-return-service/ . These are seller offers, not observed client invoices.
- Free Irish reminders https://www.businesses.ie/compliance-reminders and UK official free reminders https://www.gov.uk/guidance/register-for-email-reminders-from-companies-house ; **kill generic deadline reminders**.
- Existing integrated Irish provider claims automated B1 preparation and CORE filing https://complianceos.ie/ ; marketing claims not verified production capability, C1/C2 competitor risk, not automatically complete coverage kill.
- Irish accountant buyer testimonial says outsourcing bookkeeping and returns removed worry about missing deadlines https://kinore.com/service/register-of-beneficial-ownership/ ; genuine named testimonial but bundled full accounting, not independently verified payment for narrow sidecar.
- Court extension remedy https://cro.ie/annual-return/missed-deadlines/late-filing-fee-waiver/ involves court application, affidavit and legal judgement; **kill unsupervised rescue automation**.

## Board verdict
**No new survivor.** Bank one *narrow anomaly* only: pre-submission completeness/receipt assurance for Irish CRO B1, where missing signatures and accounts have an objectively documented consequence. Not outreach-ready: no buyer evidence this is the cause of enough paid penalties, no demonstrated integration-free access to filing status, no independent reason to pay alongside accountant and free reminders, no first-ten named prospects. Need actual rejection counts by reason and payer interviews before further promotion. Do not confuse €8.7m in penalties with a software TAM.

No outreach, build, merge, spending. PR #3 remains draft.

## Four highest-interest comparators

### 1. Creative Margin Engine — best relative **new acquisition-first hypothesis**, *not* current promoted survivor
Historical September concept (NOT current V3 approval): 5–50 person creative/design/production/marketing agencies; ingest quotes, historical actual hours and costs to produce source-traceable project/client profitability, price floors, scope drift and future estimate benchmarks. Entry product: a bounded **past 20 jobs Margin Leak Audit**. Existing outbound source groups: Irish creative/advertising members https://iapi.ie/members/ and Design Business Association directory of 350+ UK agencies https://www.dba.org.uk/ . Broad later expansion possible into other project-service firms, but not automatically.

**Positive vs contractor service:** buyers care about cash margin, audit can be largely on existing exports rather than document chasing/cert collection; clearer recurring job if owners need month-to-month quote-vs-actual margin checks; marketing CTA can present audit findings from voluntarily provided anonymised inputs (not fabricated public "margin loss"). US specialist Parakeeto offers *exact tech-enabled service* (not standalone software): starting $4,999 one-time Profit Assessment, $1,499/month Profit Management, data pulled/cleaned/reported from existing tools (https://www.parakeeto.com/pricing/ and https://www.parakeeto.com/faq/). This is strong **price + model evidence** but not independent transaction proof or entrant selection.

**Kill remains serious:** Productive https://productive.io/profitability/ and Scoro https://support.scoro.com/hc/en-us/articles/16489101116045-Reports-library already do profit-by-project/client, budget forecasts, client hours and margins; Parakeeto does the complete done-for-you interpretation. Inconsistent time tracking, sensitive customer financial data, costing assumptions and human CFO interpretation could defeat low-touch software. Generic Claude + spreadsheet competes with an initial single audit. No named payer for *our* hypothetical service, no buyer willingness to switch, no proved £/€ price, no proven website conversion. **Best to test before contractor** because potential scalable revenue and identifiable agencies—not permission to build.

Cheap bounded falsifier: Can 5–10 firms with at least 20 completed scoped projects and reliable time/quote data independently demonstrate a margin question **not answered** by their existing tools and choose/pay for an external fixed-scope historical audit (price tested, not asserted) without migrating PSA? Two respondents willing to provide securely redacted permitted samples before any code or financial-data intake.

## Deep attack — console certification preflight

**Buyer and trigger:** an indie studio or publisher approaching PlayStation/Xbox/Nintendo submission with a release-candidate build.

**Economic evidence:** Tokkun advertises compliance pre-certification from €9,500. Kudos says a failed submission can delay release by weeks. Microsoft itself offers optional paid certification coverage, including light and full passes.

**Real access:** the [TIGA directory](https://www.tiga.org.uk/directory), [Ukie membership directory](https://ukie.org.uk/membership-directory/) and [UK Games Map](https://ukie.org.uk/uk-games-map) expose many named studios and publishers. This proves reachability, not unmet need.

**AI test:** an agent can automate repeatable controller, suspend/resume, locale and save-state scenarios. It cannot safely derive confidential platform requirements from public material, obtain platform approval, cover long-play/network edge cases, or replace hardware/manual certification experience.

**Second kill:** the supposed software wedge either ignores the authoritative private rules or requires platform credentials, dev kits, build access, CI integration and specialist support. Microsoft and QA firms can absorb the automation. No low-liability standalone entry remains.

**Verdict:** KILLED.

## Deep attack — factory-ready apparel tech packs

**Buyer and trigger:** a clothing founder or brand launching a new style, changing factory or documenting an existing sample.

**Economic evidence:** services range from £79/style to roughly £175–£549/style; Techpacker begins at $35/user/month and AI Tech Packs lists $150/user/month. The job recurs across styles and collections.

**Real access:** [UKFT's member list](https://ukft.org/who-we-are/about-us/members), [British Brand Accelerator directory](https://makeitbritish.co.uk/british-brand-accelerator-client-directory/) and UK apparel directories expose named brands/manufacturers. They are category access, not proof of dissatisfaction.

**AI test:** AI can draft flats, BOMs and construction notes. The risky fields are exact measurements, tolerances, grade rules, fabric behaviour, stitch/machine compatibility and factory-specific availability. A polished pack that invents those facts creates extra sampling, not a complete result.

**Second kill:** if the product asks the buyer to supply every production-safe value, it is a document builder competing at $35/month. If it supplies those values, it becomes a technical-design service with liability and revisions. Exact services already sell the finished pack for £79.

**Verdict:** KILLED.

## Deep attack — influencer compliance evidence

**Buyer and trigger:** a brand or agency approving creator posts before publication and retaining an audit trail.

**Demand evidence:** the ASA's 2024 monitoring reviewed more than 50,000 items; about 57% of sampled influencer ads complied, 9% used inadequate labels and 34% had no disclosure. Brands, creators and agencies share responsibility.

**Exact competition:** Clearlabel advertises campaign rosters and evidence packs; PinkSpider, Castlytics and GetGenAI advertise automated influencer/ASA review and approval logs. The ASA publishes free guidance explaining prominent upfront disclosure.

**Real access:** the [Influencer Marketing Trade Body register](https://imtb.org.uk/register-members/) and [IPA agency list](https://ipa.co.uk/membership/our-members/ipa-member-agencies/) expose named agencies. No claim is made that listed agencies want another tool.

**AI test:** a grounded model can flag label wording/placement and produce an audit record. That makes the feature easy for campaign-management incumbents to add. Substantive claim substantiation, sector rules and edge cases need legal/compliance review.

**Second kill:** the narrow deterministic layer is directly occupied and partly free; broadening into complete compliance creates high update, liability and support burdens.

**Verdict:** KILLED.

- **CertScout:** only the StartupBasket idea page surfaced for the console-product concept. An unrelated German company shares the name. Treat the page as an idea/competitor claim, not a live product or demand proof.

- **UK Clothing Factory £79 tech pack:** corroboration returned only the provider's own pages.

These sources may evidence a published offer and price. They do not establish customers, satisfaction, revenue or market size. The corresponding kills do not depend on claimed traction: multiple independent alternatives and the founder-fit/ownership failures are sufficient.

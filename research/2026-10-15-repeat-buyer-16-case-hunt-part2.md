### RB09 — Web-development agency purchases personalised B2B outreach execution
- **Source:** [buyer-authored Upwork manual outreach request, 10 Oct 2026](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Personalized-B2B-Lead-Generation-Outreach-Specialist-Manual-Cold-Email-LinkedIn-Agency_~022108876523512456637/), $120 per month advertised starting budget, markets include UK, EU, US, Canada and Australia; buyer geography not independently confirmed.
- **Complete result:** validated relevant B2B decisionmakers plus individualised outreach, replies classified, warm introductions for sales team. **Buyer expressly prohibits mass automation/automated LinkedIn**; human empathy, messaging quality and trust are central. **M2/S0–S1**. Apollo/Hunter/NeverBounce can supply data, but cannot supply accepted positive commercial replies. **C2/D1**. **REJECT** low fee and buyer explicitly buys manual execution, not mass lead tool.

## Honest count and source limitations
RB01–RB16 represent **16 distinct requested economic jobs**, each with a buyer-side public request and complete service being sought. **They do not establish 16 replicated independent markets or 16 paid fulfilled transactions.** RB07 and RB03 each have two same-buyer listings; counted once each. RB08 includes two independent buyer companies for related but not identical manual link-acquisition outcomes. RB11 has two independent reporting needs but no proof repeat accepted same product.
All cases are separate unnumbered exploration and have not passed full V3.3 outreach/technical gates; no seller contacts, bids, interviews, invoices, source file downloads or purchases. Any posted case budget is M2 at most absent award/payment; for RB07 first job award status is separately marked, but **award is not a verified invoice**.

## Commercial second-kill across promising clusters
1. **Training registration (RB01 + RB02):** independent different buyers, but *different* bought results; [Arlo registrations+invoicing](https://www.arlo.co/features/course-registration-software), [Arlo Xero](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/360031362652-Xero-integration-overview), [Arlo approval rules](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/360000629886-Registration-rules-overview) are exact broad substitutes. No durable entrant difference beyond customised n8n/WordPress process, which is agency work. No customer first-ten cohort at proposed price.
2. **Trade-credit onboarding (RB03):** Citipost advertised £1k then £2k for same outcome; repeat **one buyer**. [Credit Intell digital application](https://www.creditintell.co.uk/products/digital-application) handles fully branded applications and risk assessment. Implementation of Salesforce, legal signatures and credit terms remains heavy. Not separately paid low-touch software.
3. **Shop drawings (RB07):** first £25 project **awarded**, £20 new brief, SAME manufacturer buyer. Even if accepted repeated demand, [DriveWorks Solo automated manufacturing drawings](https://www.driveworks.co.uk/products/driveworkssolo/) and technician QA are direct substitutes. ~200–250 jobs/month at advertised fee merely to reach £5k gross, before software/cloud/support, without independently verifiable customer count.
4. **Renewals migration (RB04):** [Maxio-HubSpot connector](https://docs.maxio.com/selling/hubspot/overview/understand-the-hubspot-integration) plus [HubSpot native renewal quoting](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/quotes/create-a-renewal-quote-on-a-contract) covers purchase lifecycle. Customer still commissions migration of historic dirty legacy data. Previously killed automated implementation must not be revived.
5. **SEO accepted placements (RB08):** TWO independent buyers do purchase *similar categories*, but acceptable output requires site owner/editorial approval and trust. Software can propose sites and track placements, not deliver the independent published link. Cost $50/$250 varies; latter buyer Lithuanian. No founder-operable independent closed deliverable.
6. **Prospect enrichment (RB10):** £65 listed job, buyer wants LinkedIn named profiles, and generic AI can attempt research. API entitlements and freshness are expensive; prior technographic-prospect tools at $250+/month are exact incumbents. More specialized source filters only create tiny feature wedge.

## Decision
**16 exploratory screens completed.** 14 direct reject / existing graveyard collision (including RB07 roller-shutter drawings), 1 RESEARCH-WATCH ONLY (RB01 admission-and-payment), 1 REFERENCE ONLY (RB15 free-preview distribution). But **no** research-watch qualifies for desk-to-outreach: no three independent buyers demonstrably purchasing same accepted complete result at workable economics, and direct competitors already supply product or purchased service. RB01 remains a purchasing request worth noting; RB07 is useful as proof *same-buyer* repeated commissioning but fails commercial fee/headroom. **0 new OUTREACH-READY, 0 BUILD-READY.**

No numbered attempt is added to historical formal 295 killed. Historical other lead and CPO status unchanged. **Stop this batch rather than narrow further**. A next materially different discovery should find actual completed externally paid repeat outputs with *multiple named independently paying businesses*, not expand generic public freelance listing counts.

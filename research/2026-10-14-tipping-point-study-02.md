## Boundary update
A valuable complete service splits into (1) cheap deterministic/AI-transformable component, (2) high-cost data access, expert judgement, sign-off, or real-world execution. The gap between prices is not available software revenue. The unproven middle is a complete digitally deliverable outcome with independent budget, objectively testable acceptance and a buyer motivated to pay alongside incumbents.

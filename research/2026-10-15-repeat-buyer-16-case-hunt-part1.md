# Repeated-buyer purchase hunt — 16 cases, Part 1 (RB01–RB08)
**Date:** 2026-10-15 | **Status:** bounded exploratory buyer/job screen, not numbered V3 attempts #961–1060. No outreach, spending or production code.

## Operating rules / precise meaning of count
- Each RB case is one distinct **buyer + purchase trigger + requested complete outcome**. Multiple jobs posted by the SAME buyer count as one buyer cluster; two sources do not imply two buyers. Cases are **buyer-authored requests**, not merely vendor prices. A job listing gives a posted intended budget, not an actual payment unless the listing separately shows awarded.
- Geography Ireland → UK → EU; external buyers marked as analogue, not local market proof. Distinct complete outcome even when sector repeats; repeats with existing ledger listed and NOT promoted or resurrected.
- M (spend evidence) and S (independently capturable new-entrant completed digital outcome) per V3.5/V3.6. M2: posted budget; M1: buyer indicates wish to procure but no budget; M3: observed third-party award/repeat selection, not automatically payment; S1: hypothesised separate new-entrant job. C/D and AI are assessed deeply only for stronger prospects, not misleadingly invented for every screen.
- **15–20 cases authorised implicitly by "another run"**. This round includes 16 distinct buyer/job screens, *not 16 independent repeated-payment markets*. Archived search pages may show stale relative time labels: capture explicit 2026 dates when given; platform listing statuses at search, not independently audited invoices.
- No automatic changes to the 295 formally KILLED prior-numbered board, CPO six-email status, or historical banked/anomaly findings. Explicitly exclude family organisation and photo management.

### RB01 — Ireland clinical training academy: screened course booking through staged payments and accounts
- **Source:** [Irish buyer via transformation consultant, Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/n8n-Automation-Build-Course-Registration-Payments-Admin-for-Clinical-Training-Academy_~022109998575924600733/), October 2026 listing; request for formal eight-workstream EUR quote, **no actual quote or award proved**.

Present job: screening credentials, provisional offer, limited seats, Stripe deposit and balance, Xero receipt/invoice, calendar, reminders, cohort certificate/email, owner handover. Uses WordPress, Stripe, Google Workspace and Xero; asks not to replace with full LMS.
- **M1 (open-price procurement), S1** proposed independent software. Independent second demand? **RB02 is a different education provider but does not purchase the same full course-screening/staged-payment job**. Not independent exact-job convergence.
- **Competition / C2–C3:** [Arlo course registration](https://www.arlo.co/features/course-registration-software), [Arlo native Xero](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/360031362652-Xero-integration-overview), [Arlo registration rules](https://support.arlo.co/hc/en-gb/articles/360000629886-Registration-rules-overview); [Bookwhen Stripe](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/2989336-integrating-with-stripe) but [Bookwhen Xero integration limitations](https://support.bookwhen.com/en/articles/13684770-faq-how-to-integrate-stripe-payments-in-bookwhen-with-xero). A limited integration gap may exist for Bookwhen, but **Arlo covers the overall end-to-end job** and seller requested n8n custom service.
- **D2** technically documented APIs; acceptance/exception testing and owner approval still necessary. **Repeat-AI:** n8n+prebuilt integrations+general AI can create much of the sequence, client-specific credentials and QA remain. **AI-build:** entrants can build templates as easily as this founder. **Second kill:** adoption of a separate paid system adds more complexity than paying an integrator once. No first-ten matching firms proven. **Verdict: RESEARCH WATCH (buyer budget unpriced and repeat independent buyer unproven), not OUTREACH-READY**.

## Fast commercial challenge of the strongest-looking purchase request
**P01 UK agency client-onboarding automation, $300 job post:** its specified end-to-end result is Stripe/ManyRequests sale→ client/account creation → Asana task → Google Drive folder → reporting/alerts. [Source brief](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Code-Automation-Engineer-Zapier-ManyRequests-Asana-Looker-Studio-Integration_~022107807005062416673/).
- **Exact incumbent counter-proof:** [Zapier's live ManyRequests integration page](https://zapier.com/apps/manyrequests/integrations) already displays templates for Stripe subscriptions creating ManyRequests clients, ManyRequests requests creating Asana tasks, and client-created Drive folders. Zapier supplies no-code workflow creation and MCP actions. This is **C2+ exact accessible incumbent**, not speculative competition.
- **Repeat-AI / AI-build:** with vendor templates and a low-code workflow builder, an agency can repeatedly operate configured automations; an AI-assisted integrator can readily build the basic sequence. Error handling/bespoke cross-tool consent may still require client-specific support; that burden is not clearly separable into a low-touch independent product.
- **S grade remains S1** for new standalone software; one fixed $300 buyer job is demand for a bespoke *implementation*, not a monthly standalone application. First-ten qualified buyer group missing and adoption friction of adding independent product vs using the templates is high.
- **Revised verdict: do not promote P01 to deep product investigation on this evidence**. Only direct evidence of several independent recurring payments for a *completed* repeatable operational service with a distinct customer-selection reason could justify revisiting; avoid arbitrarily narrowing to one named agency stack.

## The genuinely useful positive controls
- **R05 SuitShop + Shopify + Loop Returns + TryNow:** [named merchant case](https://www.shopify.com/ie/case-studies/suitshop) demonstrates paid independent apps can coexist with a huge incumbent checkout; but already-owned outcomes are not product vacancies.
- **R01 Standby Self Storage moves near 1,000 customers to Stora** and **R02 Thompson Leisure 50 vehicles across IE/NI chooses Prohire** show firms do replace satisfactory-looking incumbents when whole-business implementation/performance improves. These are supplier case studies, not invoices; demonstrate commercial *selection mechanism*, but founder feasibility is poor for recreating the operating systems.
- **C01 Shopify Shop MCP 13 October 2026** is real new distribution technology with [official SDK documentation](https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/add-shopping-and-shop-pay-checkout-to-any-agent-with-shop-mcp), but already owns catalogue/checkout. No evidence merchants are paying separate suppliers for a completed app resulting from this event.
- **A01 545 Irish SEAI solar firms** is real public reach, not a demonstrated paying cohort. A02 32 OSS providers too constrained for assumed broad market.
- **M02 Anchor Hanover £37,500 Minute Writer payment** is a rare official documented cash transfer for a *software product*, but this is paid to Board Intelligence and governance/security needs are significant. Treat it as evidence of the product class, not cheap clone opportunity.

## Method evaluation and changes for subsequent discovery
1. **Separate observation metrics from opportunity metrics.** Directory entries, aggregate spending and vendor case studies are controls, not independent cases passing a buyer+paid completed job gate. The earlier 30-case run counted 20 supplier/fee comparators; this round added real switching and timing, but 24/36 observations still do not individually prove a payable new entrant job.
2. **Make candidate seeding conditional on a buying cluster.** Prefer >=2 independent buyers authoring near-identical purchase decisions; before deep research seek third independent transaction / buyer-side account / completed project. Keep single buyer jobs as weak signals.
3. **Trace spend to a separately valuable complete outcome.** Use existing V3.6 S levels for promotion, but during discovery allow a *testable selection hypothesis* when tangible buyer spend is otherwise proved; do not conflate hypotheses with S3 buyer selection.
4. **Priority allocation for any further bounded screen:** favour ~50% buyer-authored paid commissions, buyer-verified paid add-ons and actual purchases, ~20% named incumbent-switch decisions, ~15% meaningful dated economics changes, ~15% unusual distribution/transacting. Use official directories only as *support* for qualified cases, not as six of six case quotas.
5. **Run exact incumbent substitutions early but not merely competitor landing-page kills.** P01 was weakened by a *working templated* Zapier workflow matching the exact buyer's requested outcome, which is stronger C2 than generic 'Zapier exists'.
6. **Do not invent a final survivor.** Search discovery improvement is judged by proportion of evidence-rich independent buyer clusters, not arbitrary number of candidate names, and eventually by buyer conversion.

## Decision
This 36-observation bounded cycle is **closed**. No new independent completed paid digital outcome with first-ten reach and acceptable founder cost has passed the standing V3 commercial gate. **Zero OUTREACH-READY and BUILD-READY**. The study *does* demonstrate a better discovery process: M4 actual payments, M2 direct buyer purchase requests, source-dated economics, named customer switching and distribution cohorts are now separated instead of mashed into 'market potential'.

**Next highest-information move** (conditional on another user-led run): run a demand-cluster pass across *multiple independent buyers paying for the same accepted complete output*, with incumbent template checks before product shaping. Do not default back to random 100 or expensive professional services, and don't revive prior kills without materially new buyer evidence.

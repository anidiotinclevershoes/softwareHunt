## Directional kills / no-promotion observations
1. **Wet hire plant off-hire:** DatumPlant already markets late off-hire alerts, ticket capture and invoice checking. A generic replica has no evidenced selection advantage.
2. **Breed cattle management:** ICBF and Agrinet already serve breeding data, advice and recordkeeping at published prices. Avoid generic herd management without a buyer-specific switch trigger.
3. **Breed seed royalties:** BIPO has existing rights and collection infrastructure. Complex legal/data dependencies, not a low-friction default.
4. **Escalate financial complaints:** Financial Ombudsman rules confirm deadlines and response obligations, but a deadline calendar alone does not justify a paid product. Integration, accuracy and regulatory responsibility need testing.
5. **Meteor collectibles:** physical authentication and valuations require specialist human inspection; an AI document generator is not equivalent to an insured valuation.

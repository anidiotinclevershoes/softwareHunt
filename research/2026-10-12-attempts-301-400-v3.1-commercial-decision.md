## Commercial decision

- **New survivors:** none.
- **New anomalies:** none retained. The strongest raw pain—creator tax-form holds, invalid-traffic deductions, parcel surcharges, variable holiday pay and grant payment conditions—failed on system ownership or exact operating competitors.
- **Real customer access:** strong public routes exist for design agencies, publishers, funded arts organisations, payroll professionals, cinemas, climbing walls, trampoline parks, pottery studios, chocolatiers, cheesemakers and literary agents. Access did not repair the product economics.
- **Directional lesson:** V3.1 artefact-first searching improved consequence evidence, but this tranche again found that the consequential answer usually belongs to the platform, payroll, funder, carrier or professional signatory.
- **Board:** unchanged. No promotion to ANOMALY, BANKED, DESK-VALIDATION, OUTREACH-READY or BUILD-READY. Existing CPO/quarry/GP states remain separate and unchanged.

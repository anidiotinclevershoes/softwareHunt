# Integrated 36-case discovery experiment — proposal and one-off operating protocol
**Dated:** 2026-10-15 | **Status:** user-instructed exploratory experiment, NOT permanent V3.8 approval and NOT an amendment overriding AGENTS / V3–V3.7.

## Why change
Previous money-first and acquisition-first screens found real payments but repeatedly inferred the wrong thing from them. The next research pass must integrate the full history *at discovery time*:
- V3.1 primary economic artefact first, trace money/payer/job, not keyword `software`.
- V3.2 entrant must offer a complete useful outcome; incumbents may evidence demand and still defeat entry.
- V3.3 buyer reach, first-ten and low-friction falsification before promotion, but desk-supported selection hypothesis is not entrant-specific buyer commitment.
- V3.4 documented execution + verified acceptance + safe exceptions; founder need not retrain as specialist.
- V3.5 premium-convenience attribution (what portion is delegable work vs expertise/physical activity).
- V3.6 M0–M4 (economic evidence) and S0–S4 (capturable independently bought complete digital output) are DISTINCT; do not downgrade those promotion gates at discovery.
- Research A: verified independent first buyers, referral/consultant channels, app marketplaces, intercepting incumbent-alternative searches; negative controls for churn/poor margins/false app installs.
- Research B: real 2026 economics and distribution changes, before/after date, change *event* vs announcement, not treating attention as new spend.
- Market Entry Gradient: broad competitive markets can admit entrants; do not hunt permanently tiny gaps between two competitors.
- Late 2026 buyer-first screens: buyer-authored briefs > generic vendor rate cards, but client brief != paid award and custom script != SaaS.
- Unexpected seeds are good for *source diversity*, not semantic-coincidence demand (100 frozen V3.7 attempts yielded zero two-route convergence).
- Explicit current exclusions: family organisation and photo management / adjacent variants. Do not silently change legacy historical statuses.

## Six independent entry mechanisms — six genuinely different buyer/job transactions each
1. **Money movement:** actual payments, buyer awards, repeat agency outsourcing, paid outcome evidence; prioritize smaller accessible counterparties over megacontracts.
2. **Active buyer intent:** explicitly budgeted requests, requests for replacement, direct buyers discussing paid alternatives and acceptance conditions; not ten near-identical Upwork job posts.
3. **Changed economics:** recent specific 2026 fee/policy/distribution/capability change with dated implementation; prove the economic consequence, not an imagined new SaaS budget.
4. **Buyer access:** reachable buyer populations and actual route to intercept buying; distinguish a directory from a channel where an entrant can sell.
5. **Divergent transactions:** unconventional product/service handoffs, timed value, high-ticket conveniences, physical-to-digital outcome where software may finish a distinct independently paid result; no arbitrary noun combinations.
6. **Established-market re-entry:** named customer switches/case evidence, channel partner sales, viable distinct cost/implementation/distribution advantage, rather than a feature no incumbent bothered adding.

## Primary record for EACH of 36 rows
- Case ID I01–I36; stream; **source URL and published/event date**; direct buyer and purchase trigger; M0–M4 grade (seller fee vs actual transaction distinguished); whether real purchase or just request.
- Purchased complete outcome and plausible digital bounded outcome; spend attributable to independently replaceable work vs expertise/physical/platform advantage (S0–S4).
- Ireland/UK/EU applicability and realistic first-ten access (only name actual prospects when supported).
- Adjacent/directional graveyard collision check; incumbents and alternative with C0–C4; D0–D4 for credible documentation/acceptance; repeated AI and AI-built-entry threats.
- Selection hypothesis **separate** from buyer proof; economic headroom (how many customers/transactions for ~€5k monthly at **labelled hypothetical** price); adoption burden; strongest second kill.
- Verdict must be one of DUPLICATE/REJECT / EVIDENCE INCOMPLETE / RESEARCH WATCH / DEEPER COMMERCIAL TEST; those are exploratory labels, not the formal board.

## Research operations
- Six streams × six **distinct economic jobs**, not six vendor examples each. Favor independent primary artefacts; after at most two targeted attempts lacking primary evidence mark INCOMPLETE.
- At least two independent source routes needed to call something repeatable; without it an isolated paid brief is only a signal.
- Do not treat press coverage, tender budget ceiling, client testimonials, job posts and buyer invoices interchangeably.
- Avoid falsely inflating a source count: researching a direct competitor or looking at six search queries is one case, not six.
- At most eight for deeper commercial pass, at most four for adversarial final investigation. No quota of survivors. Active CPO work is separate.
- **Close at 36 distinct records OR honestly report a partial count if evidence/time limits stop execution.** Zero survivors is valid; don't rescue one through micro-narrowing.
- Do not contact people, submit proposals, purchase subscriptions, write product code, alter main or merge PR #3.

A candidate can graduate to a **RESEARCH WATCH** on two independent buyer requests plus plausible selection, but it does **not** become OUTREACH-READY unless it independently passes all existing V3.3 gates incl. ten matching named prospects, commercial materiality, second-kill, AI, acceptance and founder-fit. A buyer promise/demo/payment remains the separate BUILD-READY gate.

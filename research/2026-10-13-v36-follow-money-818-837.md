## Biggest finding — #825 independent digital photo organising from €1,000+
**Who actually offers the complete service:** The Photo Gatherer in Ireland openly lists fully digital organising **from €1,000** (a second live price page segments at €995 for up to 10,000 images, €1,750 for up to 25,000, €2,500+ for 50,000+; quote scope may differ). It says a collection typically takes **20–40 hours** to gather, review and organise. [Service and prices](https://www.thephotogatherer.com/digital-organising) and [separate banded pricing](https://www.thephotogatherer.com/pricing). UK [Clear Bubble](https://www.clearbubble.co.uk/digital) offers **£960 / £1,560 / £2,040** packages by source count. This is an unusual **individual paying for complete high-ticket convenience** and a very different payment band from typical small online utilities.

**Full purchase:** customer exports photos from multiple phones, iCloud/Google Photos, drives/USBs; service returns one accessible original-quality archive, duplicate/outlier review, chronological or event organisation and a safe 3-2-1 backed-up library. Do **not** substitute a new photo viewer or cloud storage; the outcome includes source collection, handover, restore and reassurance. **M2**, independently priced twice, **S2**, not confirmed buyer invoice. Named reviews on provider sites are seller-hosted; this is *not* S3 buyer selection.

**Why it might theoretically be software:** local-first reversible importer; original file manifests/checksums; duplicate hashing and perceptual similarity grouping; metadata date recovery; format-preserving event grouping; export with CSV mapping; backup/restore test. Those components are documentable and objectively verifiable (D3 for bytes, hash reconciliation and backups; not D3 for subjective event meaning or photo preference). Could use a desktop/offline application to avoid transferring private family images to founder infrastructure; support burden remains.

**Adversarial second kill:** [Mylio Photos](https://support.mylio.com/how-much-does-mylio-photos-cost) already sells cross-device organisation and backup from **$25/month** and [deduplication](https://support.mylio.com/removing-camera-roll-duplicates-in-mylio-photos); Apple/Google Photos, Immich and local dedup utilities already address many steps. The remaining €1k fee may buy **the professional doing it, managing confusing phone/cloud exports, deciding which of near-duplicate memories matters, and guaranteeing nothing is lost**. It may be the least likely type of buyer to accept standalone software with set-up effort. Data custody/security, backups, very large collections and one-off acquisition are high risk.

**Decision: BANKED lead only** (economic anomaly in convenience spending, not a survivor). Cheapest falsification **before any build:** find three independent customers who actually paid ≥€750 for *digital-only* organising; learn what prevented DIY software adoption, whether they would buy an owner-operated offline tool below €250, and how much expert involvement was needed. No outreach authorised or performed. If all prefer a done-for-you human regardless of tool, kill outright. Do not conflate this with photo-book printing or existing standalone private photo software ideas.

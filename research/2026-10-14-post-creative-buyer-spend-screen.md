## Three deeper adversarial checks
1. **X01 strongest buyer-defined job.** Could be sold as a fixed-price automated course deliverable *implementation*, but not yet a recurring product: single buyer wants custom integration, budget £500, no evidence learner programme sold the document as a separate service, output semantics, which training provider category, customer deployment quality or whether cheap Document/AI assessment platforms already meet requirements. UK training buyer pool is *findable* via CIPD study-centre directory (example filtered search reported 104 entries https://findacentre.cipd.org/search/p11?country=United+Kingdom&qualification=4748&qualificationType=cipd ) and coaching/training sites, but most are NOT demonstrated to deliver the target personal multiple-document output. Need 10 actual matching buyers with documented workbook→completed action plan behaviour, not just 10 schools. **Do not promote even to OUTREACH-READY.** Specific stop question: 'Show the last paid client's completed workbook and the exact set of documents you then manually assemble; who pays you for it and why haven't Cluelabs/Documint handled it?'
2. **X02 council retrieval** direct buyer project is valuable proof someone is commissioning custom work, yet PPH price *explicitly auto-generated*, and GovChat/PlanningAlerts/PlanningIndex already address source-linked intelligent planning information and notifications. Could possibly build bespoke *for this one commissioner* but their business model and maintenance is unknowable. Generic standalone entrant killed. Don't rescue via yet another single-council exception.
3. **X03 credit watch** actual buyer wants credit score changes and insolvency notices, but Creditsafe explicitly supplies portfolios, scores, notices, exports, API. Without licenced scoring data our output is incomplete; with it, commoditised reseller + cost/support burden. Kill despite posted development brief.

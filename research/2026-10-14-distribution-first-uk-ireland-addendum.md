# Distribution-first pilot — Ireland/UK diversification, 2026-10-14

## Scope
Targeted buyer-intent and paid digital-work search, deliberately avoiding prior ecommerce/CRM focus. This is an **exploratory evidence addendum**, not 15 fully audited V3 cases or new #961–1060 attempts. Evidence from live public listings is provisional; an "awarded" marker does not prove final settlement, renewal or a SaaS budget.

## Emerging method insight
- Prioritize **buyer-authored fixed-scope awarded briefs** over seller listings and founder-led community posts. They reveal what was commissioned, budget and acceptance criteria. Awarded != paid and does not imply recurring demand.
- Next require evidence that a buyer repeats the *same job* on a cadence, not just repeats input updates after purchasing a one-off template. The distinction is decisive for €5k MRR.
- Search biases: marketplaces overrepresent cheap bespoke spreadsheets and old postings; no outreach-ready prospect list or customer interviews.

## Verdict
9 grounded signals including one excluded founder-led discussion, 0 new survivors, 0 formal board changes, 0 completed full V3 commercial validations. Frozen #961–1060 untouched. Strongest countercase: a cheap custom spreadsheet or freelancer already supplies the complete result and creates no subscription demand.

## Next attack
Find current Ireland/UK **repeat purchase orders, retainers or recurring service invoices** for one bounded digital outcome, and verify why an entrant could replace the spend at low adoption friction. Do not search more generic spreadsheet dashboard requests.

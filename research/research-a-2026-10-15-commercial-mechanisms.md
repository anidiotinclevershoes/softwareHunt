## Positive transfer cases
1. **Swifteq:** founder reports €40–44k MRR in 2025; HomeExchange named buyer reports 10k+ monthly Zendesk tickets automatically merged and over 40 hours monthly translation work saved. Discovery through free tools, native marketplace, Zendesk consultants and vendor employees. Mechanism: independently paid complete transaction within an existing stack. Caveat: experienced developer, platform concentration, ongoing SOC2/security/customer support. https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/building-a-51k-mo-ecosystem-of-small-apps-tvYLER4k6KVeA4NiwRJL ; https://www.swifteq.com/case-study-homeexchange
2. **Helploom:** founder reported a profitable SaaS sale, sale price undisclosed; customers discovered via Google-indexed Reddit 'Intercom alternatives' discussions rather than general startup community posts. Caveat: buyer-intent SEO / Reddit fragility and a long-run integration/function parity ceiling. https://blog.acquire.com/bootstrapped-profitable-and-acquired-in-four-days/
3. **BoloForms founders' *earlier separate SaaS*:** real initial client problem; first 10 reportedly took 2–2.5 months; an affiliate partnership with a Facebook group owner unlocked growth to ~$12k MRR before sale. BoloForms later approached $1m ARR, but founder's prior experience is not transferable. https://blog.acquire.com/how-a-successful-saas-exit-shaped-a-nearly-1m-arr-business/
4. **Timizer:** signed French consultant activity-report workflow unlocks invoicing; founder ~€1.1k MRR after years, rather than instant growth. https://timizer.io/ ; https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-i-built-a-b2b-saas-to-automate-the-worst-admin-task-for-french-freelancers-and-reached-1k-mrr-in-a-few-years-VzJRcsoG63oe5ZIkLV0a

- **Podifai:** first five customers from Google Ads but acquisition economics unattractive; >400 free installs with $0 current revenue Jul 2026. It is an early underperformer, not a confirmed closure. https://podifai.com/founders-journey/i-may-never-get-a-job-again

- **Vibe AI:** named 2026 founder postmortem of unsustainable retention and AI margins, promotional source; not a matched B2B comparison. https://gravity.fast/blog/vibe-ai-postmortem/

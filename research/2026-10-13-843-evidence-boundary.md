## Additional priced market information

- Irish [AllyJen](https://allyjen.ie/) currently advertises **€39.99/month + VAT self-managed** allergen technology and **€79.99/month + VAT fully managed** menu and ingredient updates, including 14 allergen tracking. This demonstrates **M2** that Irish food venues can buy a separately priced managed digital menu/allergen maintenance workflow.

- [FSAI MenuCal](https://www.fsai.ie/business-advice/running-a-food-business/caterers/about-menucal) is a free source-of-record calculator for recipes/allergens; FSAI says food businesses must verify supplied ingredients and update allergen information as menus and suppliers change.
- UK [Kitchen Tonic](https://www.kitchentonic.com/services/consulting) advertises £200 for a full-menu allergen matrix and £25 per additional dish, a closer **UK priced professional-output analogue**. UK food rules/customer context may differ from Irish customer; a UK offered price does not prove the Irish Nutricount professional service has an observable Irish fee or a buyer-paid invoice.

## Boundary and decision

**Do not substitute the €79.99/month AllyJen managed menu maintenance price for Nutricount's independently verified recipe-analysis/ingredient signoff.** The former publishes supplied customer facts and updates; it does not demonstrate qualified third-party assessment of unknown recipes, cooking cross-contact or nutrition correctness.

Therefore #843 remains **evidence-INCOMPLETE** rather than being falsely killed for paid demand or promoted on a price attached to a different service. This is a useful negative control for V3.6: there is local willingness-to-offer managed convenience, but neither independent fee for the exact full specialist job nor a new software entrant advantage. The FSAI's existing free official workflow is the strongest existing alternative. No contact with vendors, no quotes requested, no build.

**Further economical resolution:** obtain one real Irish buyer invoice or written quote for complete menu-wide professional nutrition/allergen verification (not a QR menu maintenance plan), then do the entrant-selection second-kill. Until then, #843 remains M1/S1 and INCOMPLETE. Formal 100 cases attempted / 99 verdict resolved unchanged.

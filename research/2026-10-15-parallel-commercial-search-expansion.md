This **demonstrates real independently paying buyers and repeat purchases**, but *explicitly kills* a near-identical new low-ticket bookings product at the founder's €5k–€10k/month target without proven scalable distribution or markedly larger spend. Named multiple buyers of an existing product don't imply payment for a new vendor.

### B. Strong actual software switches, weak solo-founder takeover
Named customer switching/adoption examples are real but mostly **vendor-authored**, so neither purchase amounts nor unbiased customer satisfaction are verified: Black Sheep Vets/ezyVet; Elevation Recruitment/Tracker; Three Queens Hotel/InnCloud; Sandwell bereavement services/PlotBox; Hexa self-storage/Kinnovis. The recurring purchase mechanism is **whole-business outcome replacement** under operator-specific migration and support. Shortcuts such as a narrow add-on, lower price or AI-generated CRUD screen would not meet the current purchased result.

### C. Dated regulatory trigger — disproportionate competition
England and Wales' receipt-of-waste rule took effect **1 October 2026**, and requires recording permitted-site receipts within two working days. DEFRA publishes a long list of approved third-party providers and a 14-scenario approval process; a manual public route exists. Published third-party tiers as of 15 Oct: DutyOS £19.99+VAT, WasteLinks £25, WasteMatrix £30, Byrne £89 and ComplyWaste £99 monthly. Sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/digital-waste-tracking-check-if-you-need-to-report-the-waste-you-receive ; https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/report-receipt-of-waste-choose-a-software-provider/report-receipt-of-waste-choose-a-software-provider ; https://dutyos.co.uk/pricing ; https://www.wastelinks.co.uk/operators ; https://wastematrix.co.uk/ ; https://byrnedigitalsolutions.co.uk/pricing ; https://www.complywaste.co.uk/pricing?for=receivers . Formal gate lesson: documented D4 test path and a forced deadline can still fail market entry if competing approved low-price full products already exist. DO NOT resurrect historic generic DRS or compliance work.

### D. Industrial software: demonstrated supplier pricing, no proved new entrant advantage
Stone fabrication and cabinet systems show larger monthly price anchors than village halls: Moraware CounterGo $100/user, Systemize $120/user with 3-seat minimum in a vendor comparison; Mozaik manufacturing $125/mo, CNC $225/mo on supplier site. These complete commercial workflows include measurement, cutouts, fabrication, CNC, proofs and scheduling; a quote-only sidecar is not a complete substitute. That leaves a **market observation**, not a build thesis or an OUTREACH-READY recommendation. Exact accepted output fixtures, machine interoperability and buyer-side change motivations are still missing.

### E. Distribution economics
A live association membership list or provider directory does not mean an entrant gets first ten customers. Supplier-assisted migration and training are ubiquitous in competitively switchable markets, and a separate vendor often wins by implementation service and reputation rather than something a full-time solo founder can reliably provide at scale.

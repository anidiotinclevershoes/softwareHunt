# Software Opportunity Hunt — V3.6 Capturable Spend / Paid Digital Completion

**Approved direction:** 2026-10-13. User instructed to update the repository and run the change.
**Status:** active research-method amendment on draft research branch; does not override or silently rewrite older results.
**Purpose:** distinguish observed *money spent* from money a new independent software entrant can credibly capture. V3.5 starts with economic activity; V3.6 adds a harder bridge from market spend to a purchase available to this founder.

## Core change
Do **not** rank a case because a buyer spends £1,000 on a complete service if 90% of the fee buys human expertise, liability, physical work, distribution, negotiated access or relationships. After M-grade evidence, ask **which precise independently purchasable outcome** a low-support product would take responsibility for, and **what evidence shows someone pays for that outcome separately**.

## Spend-capture evidence ladder (S0–S4; separate from existing M, C and D grades)
- **S0 — Not separable:** fee is predominantly tied to people, assets, authority, liability or relationships. An automation feature would not replace the purchased result.
- **S1 — Hypothetically separable:** a digital subtask exists but no observable transaction for it independently; lower-priced feature alone is not a market.
- **S2 — Independently transacted:** a named, currently offered, paid digital deliverable or a separate paid add-on genuinely completes the proposed bounded job. Need exact buyer, trigger, price and acceptance outcome. This is **not** proof an entrant will win.
- **S3 — Buyer-observed selection:** an identifiable buyer-side account, review, repeated transaction, switching story, third-party procurement record or paid coexistence demonstrates paying for the separable outcome **despite** a principal system or vendor; credible and source-linked. Vendor claims alone do not qualify.
- **S4 — Entrant-specific commitment:** direct pilot/payment/deposit/credible purchase commitment for the proposed entrant, with verifiable execution and no assumed pricing. This is a prerequisite-quality signal for BUILD-READY, not a sufficient condition by itself.

## Search order
1. **Buyer spends**: locate M2–M4 fee, invoice, disclosed contract, expense, rate card or a procuring customer, before searching for products.
2. **Paid digital boundary**: identify exactly what the customer already buys *independently*, rather than inventing a tiny sidecar. Seek subcontractor invoices, agency line items, task marketplaces, buyer requests, marketplace paid add-ons, procurement award, disclosed supplier tiers. Record S grade.
3. **Implementation fit**: V3.4 D0–D4, obtainable inputs, complete output, verification oracle, refusal boundary, rights and operating liability.
4. **Competitive selection**: exact paid competitors C0–C4, principal system, friction to add a new provider, documented reason separate purchases persist. Explicitly test low-cost products and general-purpose AI with repeated cases; run the AI-build test.
5. **Distribution and purchase energy**: name precise buyer class and first-ten route; only then estimate price/MRR scenarios (never infer willingness from seller prices).
6. **Independent kill**: articulate why the buyer would keep paying current provider, in-house team, vertical incumbent or generic AI, and why an entrant cannot capture the money.

## Research allocation change
- **Prefer** demonstrably paid external *digital execution* around existing workflows: paid agency subcontracts, separate service orders, measurable overflow/backlog and independent outcome verification.
- **Deprioritise** pure regulatory penalty avoidance, premium human professional judgement, reminders, template conversions, physical tasks, unverified concierge spending and generic data reconciliation, unless new evidence directly proves a payable digital boundary.
- Preserve **five independent seed streams**: (A) company-paid delegation; (B) premium individual/prosumer convenience; (C) paid speed/white-glove differentials; (D) obscure outsourced production; (E) known principal-system coexistence. They are research lanes, not a license to count duplicative payer/jobs.
- **Deep batches of 15–20 distinct buyer-job cases** unless an explicitly requested 100 is being completed. The user has already authorised attempts 761–860; do not reset or abandon that authorization. Work in bounded batches and certify IDs only when the existing evidence/AI/second-kill fields and 40/30/30 composition can be audited. Interim probes belong in dated research files and do not inflate certified attempt numbers.
- Expedite negative decisions: S0/S1 or exact low-cost incumbent C2+ kills before spending days on implementation. S2+ justifies deeper scrutiny, **not promotion**. S3 with usable implementation documentation and a first-ten route may justify a V3.3 bounded OUTREACH-READY test; every other gate still applies. S4 and demonstrated delivery support, but do not automatically confer, BUILD-READY.

## Test landing page copy — NOT deployed
**Headline:** A clearer picture of your project's handover paperwork — before the deadline.

**Subheading:** We organise the records you've already got, identify what's missing, and prepare a traceable handover register for your mechanical project. No new project-management system to learn. Every missing certificate stays flagged, not guessed.

**Who it is for:** Mechanical, ventilation, commercial heating and building-services subcontractors juggling more than one project. For document coordination only — technical approvals remain with your responsible project team.

**What you receive in the initial paid review:**
1. A document inventory cross-referenced to your supplied client requirements and asset list.
2. An outstanding-evidence register showing what's missing, who needs to supply it and any uncertain matches.
3. A correctly indexed sample issue section with original-file links and a list of items requiring your sign-off.

**Proposed initial offer:** One-project handover review from €350–€450 ex VAT, subject to a scoped sample. Indicative experimental price only — do not publish as established without confirmed delivery capacity and terms.

**How it works:** Discuss project scope -> agree file-handling terms -> share a redacted sample securely -> receive an exact quote -> review sample output -> decide whether to commission a full pack.

**Call to action:** 'Talk through one project's document gaps' / 'See a sample register' (no unauthorised file uploads). Contact form collects business contact and voluntary opt-in separately; do not imply consent by default.

**Important exclusions:** We do not produce or sign missing engineering certificates, perform tests, assume PSDP/PSCS duties, guarantee client approval or substitute for technical review.

**Proof needed before publishing:** sample constructed entirely from authorised/synthetic records, test target SLA and correction rate, privacy and document retention rules, human-in-the-loop QA and commercially sound turnaround.

## Sales discovery: 5 exact questions, no pitch deck
1. On your latest two commercial jobs, what were the required handover/technical-document deliverables and who signed off?
2. How many staff hours, calendar days and chasing rounds did each take, and were any externally paid?
3. What went wrong or was rejected, and what did that cost or delay? Ask for evidence, not general annoyance.
4. Who presently owns this workflow — internal document controller, manual folders, Zutec/Procore/OMLY, another outsourced service?
5. Compare an example €350–€450 external gap review vs doing nothing or using your current provider. Would you pay now for a defined one-project outcome, and why/why not? Obtain an actual paid pilot only after verifying confidentiality and scope.

## Commercial acceptance / rejection
Progress only with at least one actual buyer-paid review, client-end acceptance of a source-traceable sample, and recorded delivery effort. A second customer with similar acceptance criteria and willingness to pay is needed to infer repeatability. Hold off claiming monthly recurring revenue until actual repeat payment/contract. Stop if the new-entrant selection rationale cannot distinguish current managed suppliers, or if personal effort cannot fit alongside founder full-time work.

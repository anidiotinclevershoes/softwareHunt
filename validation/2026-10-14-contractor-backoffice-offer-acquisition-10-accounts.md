## Product hypothesis and what is for sale
Working offer: **Project Document Close-out Desk** for Irish mechanical/HVAC and adjoining specialist building-services subcontractors handling multiple commercial sites. Entry sale: fixed-scope **Handover Gap Review** for one live project, with a structured document register, source-linked presence/age/version checks, project-specified outstanding evidence list with named *responsible party*, and one worked sample issue section. If requested, separate paid **Managed Completion Pack** assembles existing supplier-issued records and tracks approved chases and revisions. Value proposition must be measured in the buyer's own rejected/delayed submission or hours, not claimed safety compliance or 'signoff guaranteed.'

Broad market (not tiny wedge): contractors generating project handover documents, technical submittals, commissioning records, supplier certificates, and maintenance evidence; first onboarding uses one project + one deliverable. Never generate certificates, engineering tests, as-builts, compliance approvals or representations that records exist; never sign or certify. Client/contractor and responsible statutory roles retain approvals. For confidentiality: client-controlled approved files, no public document uploads until storage/security controls exist.

**Proposed pricing (experiments, not demand evidence):**
- Paid gap review: **€350–€450 excl VAT** for one project, max 50 existing files / one client-supplied acceptance checklist / one review; explicit exclusions. Timebox <=5 productive hours. Refund if impossible to process? do not promise without a rule.
- Managed completion pilot **€750–€950 excl VAT per project**: capped file count and three approved supplier followups; one QA/format review and one client revision; not unconditional client acceptance. Larger scope individually priced, no fixed guarantee.

## Exact workflow + technical acceptance
1. Buyer sends permitted example project brief, existing file register or folder, client acceptance list, and 1–2 examples of approved packs. Contractually confirm client authority to share materials.
2. Map spec checklist to evidence requirements: file, item/asset ID, issuer, issue/revision, required-by and approver; classify each supplied file as present/possibly related/missing/unknown; preserve trace back to original page/file/version.
3. Human validates mapping and flags engineering judgment; no autonomous technical certification, no inferred original test data.
4. Produce named deliverables: CSV/XLSX outstanding-doc register; PDF issue index + 1 example subsection, source list; clear exception log and approval owner; optional coordination email templates drafted **only for buyer to approve**.
5. Buyer compares output to actual one-project acceptance requirements and records inaccuracies, elapsed time, outsourced handoffs, rework and purchase intention.
6. Only if accepted, scope fixed-price completion and measure end-to-end labour. Use existing PDF, spreadsheet, cloud storage, LLM transcription/text extraction where permitted; avoid building SaaS until delivery repeatability demonstrated.

Oracle: every index entry links to genuine source, no fake certs/dates, asset identifiers reconcile to supplied source, all required items either evidenced or flagged, selected revision acknowledged by named client approver. We cannot independently assert regulatory completeness without contract spec and qualified reviewer.

Irish HSA distinguishes documentary input responsibilities and safety-file oversight: https://www.hsa.ie/your_industry/construction/construction_faqs/safety_file/ . The PSDP prepares and delivers the safety file; contractors and designers provide information. Do not offer to assume PSDP/PSCS roles.

## Economics — sensitivity, not observed margins
Illustrative retainer €895/mo excl VAT ×6 accounts = **€5,370 MRR**, before founder time, VAT, support, churn, taxes. Suppose fully burdened productive document time €30/hour (hypothetical; Irish salaries €41,990 average https://ie.indeed.com/career/document-controller/salaries , not equivalent contractor billing rate) and €50/account monthly other variable costs.
- 5h/account: €895 - €150 - €50 = €695 contribution; six accounts 30h/mo + sales/management.
- 10h/account: €895 - €300 - €50 = €545; six accounts 60h/mo + sales/management.
- 18h/account: €895 - €540 - €50 = €305; six accounts 108h/mo, **unacceptable alongside full-time job**.
- QA/specialist consultation, revisions, supplier delays, customer acquisition and insurance are omitted; contribution is NOT profit.
If workflow needs specialist approvals for routine transactions or 10+ founder-hours/account, this is a service agency without SaaS economics. Low-cost rivals may price far below proposed pilot.

## Falsification / decision

What would make you switch?'
Hard stop if 5/10 say no independently paid task and no switch interest, if packages need recurrent engineering judgment/unsourced documents, or if pilot over 10 working hours repeatedly at the offered fee. No launch or promotion until **competitive selection evidence** and payment.

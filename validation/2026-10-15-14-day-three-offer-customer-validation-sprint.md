## Operating economics and acceptance standard

- Founder objective: realistic route to **>=€5,000 gross MRR** with manageable support and surplus alongside a full-time job, modest capital, Ireland first → UK → EEA. These are commercial targets, not probability or timing forecasts.
- Under V3.3, a proposition moves towards OUTREACH-READY based on a complete job, meaningful spend, independently selected reason, low-friction first-ten route, competitor and both AI tests; **BUILD-READY** still needs direct buyer commercial intent and demonstrated delivered output. One email, a survey response, a vendor rate card or a volunteered chat is not a sale.
- Use the **same script and decision capture** for all three, but do not conflate their leads, offer or success definitions. Register every contacted party once; identify 10 public matching firms per proposition (sources provided below), while noting that public fit does not mean buying intent or email permission.
- Avoid speculative pricing. Ask existing payment first. Any suggested pilot fee is explicitly a test quote subject to approval and delivery feasibility. Revenue model, not prediction: at €895/mo, 6 real monthly subscribers would produce €5,370 *gross* MRR; at €400/mo, 13 yield €5,200; at €300/mo, 17 yield €5,100; each excludes VAT, support, acquisition, outsourcing, refunds and tax. Catalogue one-offs do not count as MRR until contracted repeat work is evidenced.
- **Time-box desk research, not human reply lag:** maximum 90 minutes incremental desk research per test lane before buyer evidence. No more giant adjacent keyword batches to simulate traction. Recruitment, observation and decision logs matter more than queries.

## Reusable 5-question buyer interview scorecard (all lanes)

Conduct only through an appropriate voluntary introduction, inbound request or compliant approved channel. Record direct quotes separately from interpretations.

1. **Last concrete occurrence:** "Walk me through the last real case/project/catalogue edition when you needed this complete result—when, what input, who signed off?"
2. **Actual money and time:** "Who did it; was anyone outside your firm paid? What was the invoice/budget or exact internal hours? Can you corroborate without disclosing customer-sensitive information?"
3. **Existing alternative:** "Which vendor/system/in-house person currently finishes the job, and what is the *one* defensible reason to replace or supplement them?"
4. **Selection and exclusion:** "If a new provider delivered exactly [complete scoped job] and didn't take over your current tools, what would you need to see before using it? What would make you refuse?"
5. **Next observable commitment:** "Would you review an authorised redacted example against your actual acceptance criteria, or issue a scoped paid test order, subject to security and delivery verification? If no, why?"

Capture fields: lane; firm; buyer role; public fit URL; contact channel/lawful basis; prior contact check; outreach send date (actual, not planned); outcome; concrete recent job; paid third party yes/no/unknown; price proof (offer vs invoice); current supplier; source and complete accepted result; migration; proposed price reaction; clear yes/no; permissioned test artifact; founder work effort; next decision; date; source strength (M/S/C/D); explicit commercial status. A negative buyer response is data; silence alone is not refusal/kill.

## Decision gates

**Gate 0 — testable hypothesis (this pack):** three complete-scope propositions, preexisting market supplier evidence, buyer classes, targeted sources, an exact offer and a falsification question, historic collisions known. **PASS for pack preparation, NOT proof of buyer demand.**

**Gate 1 — buyer access:** contacted decision makers through appropriate channel and >=2 substantive real buying-job interviews per lane desired. If nobody can be reached, record a *distribution/access failure or untested state*; do not call this proof product is unwanted. CPO's previous four actual approaches count for past execution but not prospect responses.

**Gate 2 — paid selection:** one real independent business is willing to advance to a fee-specific, clearly scoped paid pilot/deposit/test contract; at least one second buyer supports same complete job/pay rationale. Interview positivity is not enough. No invented close probability or promised MRR.

**Gate 3 — founder delivery:** test 1 complete job from buyer-authorised redacted data with an acceptance checklist; record every manual step, actual costs, specialist judgement, file/integration access, QA failures, security and support. Buyer confirms receipt/acceptance in writing where possible. For contractor gap reviews initial <=5 productive founder-hours; managed <=10. Other lanes must define timeboxes from first representative job, not guess.

**Gate 4 — expansion:** recurring timing/frequency and actual repeat paid account proves a plausible path to €5k MRR net of founder workload, support and capital. No BUILD-READY without direct commercial evidence plus demonstrated accepted output.

**Hard kills (proposed entry, not historical industry):**
- 3/3 informed buyer conversations independently verify their current service/in-house method already delivers the complete proposed outcome at lower total cost and show no payable advantage, **or** an exact current full provider is preferred for substantive operational reasons.
- Primary output requires continuing licensed professional judgement, certification or sensitive unapproved data handling.
- Outcome can't be produced accurately/completely with permissioned source and the founder's realistic support budget.
- No repeat frequency or contract structure credible for an eventual €5k MRR, given acquisition/retention economics.
- Silence/no contact by sprint end = **UNTESTED**, not retroactive KILLED.

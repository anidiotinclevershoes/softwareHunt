## Why V3.5 exists

The previous run demonstrated that implementation-ready documentation is common. It did not reveal enough commercially attractive whitespace: exact low-cost products, native systems, free validators or recurring specialist approval killed most cases.

V3.5 therefore reverses the discovery order. It seeks demonstrated spending behaviour first, then asks whether a replaceable convenience component carries enough of that spend. This should expose buyers who already value saved time and lower friction while protecting against the false inference that every expensive service can become software.

No candidate status changes merely because V3.5 is active.
